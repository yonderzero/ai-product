# Real products at each level

Use these to help the product manager picture each level. One line each: what it does, and who does the work. They're well-known examples; check any you quote, since products move fast.

## 1 · System of Record: people do the work; the software stores it

- **Epic**: hospitals' patient records, orders and billing.
- **SAP**: a company's finance, supply chain and operations records.
- **Salesforce Sales Cloud**: accounts, contacts, deals and the sales pipeline.
- **Procore**: construction projects, drawings, RFIs and daily logs.

## 2 · AI Assisted: people do the work; AI suggests

- **Gmail Smart Compose**: suggests the rest of the sentence as you type.
- **GitHub Copilot code completion**: suggests the next lines of code.
- **Grammarly**: suggests clearer, correct writing.
- **Zoom AI Companion**: summarises the meeting you were in.

## 3 · AI Directed: a person asks, AI does one task, the person checks the result

- **ChatGPT and Claude**: draft, analyse or build something on request.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot**: drafts the document or deck from your files when you ask.
- **Canva Magic Studio**: creates and edits designs from a prompt.
- **Shopify Sidekick**: does a merchant's task in the store on request.

## 4 · AI Delegated: AI does the job end to end; people handle exceptions

- **Intercom Fin**: resolves customer support conversations on its own, priced per resolution.
- **Abridge**: listens to the doctor's visit and writes the clinical note the doctor signs.
- **Coding agents** (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot coding agent): take an issue and open the pull request.
- **Ramp**: reads receipts, codes expenses and checks them against policy without the employee doing it.

## 5 · AI Domain Intelligence: AI brings expertise learned from data across many customers

- **Harvey**: legal research and drafting that lawyers trust, built for law firms.
- **OpenEvidence**: answers clinicians' questions from the medical literature, with citations.
- **EvenUp**: drafts personal injury demand letters, valued from many past cases.
- **Viz.ai**: spots signs of a stroke on a scan and alerts the care team.

## 6 · System of Understanding: it understands how the domain works, anticipates and acts

- **Waymo**: understands the road well enough to drive the car, with no one at the wheel. The clearest picture of the idea, outside software.
- **Tempus**: combines clinical and molecular data across many patients to guide cancer care.
- **Palantir AIP**: models how an organisation works and acts across its operations.
- **Your vertical**: probably nobody yet. Who could get there first?

## The pattern

Going up the levels, three things change:

- **Who does the work:** from people, to people helped by AI, to AI with people handling exceptions, to AI that knows what to do before anyone asks.
- **What the product knows:** from one customer's records, to patterns across all customers, to how the whole domain behaves.
- **What customers pay for:** from seats and licences, to usage, to outcomes (per resolution, per note, per case).

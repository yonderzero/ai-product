# 05 · Build your customers

**Goal.** The team's ideal customer, built from evidence: the account they most want to win, and the three or four people in it who decide, use and resist. These personas are used for the rest of the two days.

Work in the team's product folder: the current folder, or the folder below it that has `product.md`. If there is none, run `/ai-product:start` first. Remind the team they can add files to `documents/` at any time, and read any new ones. Read `product.md`, `01-brief.md` and `04-long-list.md`. This mission runs on the `synthetic-customers` skill; follow its ground rules throughout.

## Steps

1. **Personal data check.** Scan `documents/` for people's names, emails and phone numbers. If you find any, stop and ask the team to remove them first.

2. **Map the evidence.** Run `synthetic-customers` mode 1 (map the evidence) and write `panel/evidence.md`. Show the customer types and the gaps. Ask the team what they know that the files don't, and add it tagged `[team]`.

3. **Pick the ideal customer.** Ask the team which account they most want: the one with the most pain, the money to fix it, and a reason to choose them. Run mode 2 to write `panel/icp.md`.

4. **Write the people.** Still in mode 2, build the buyer, the user and the sceptic (plus one from the edge of the market if the team wants), each with its evidence log. Show the roster table, including the share `[assumed]` per persona.

5. **Reality-check.** Assign one persona to each team member. Ask each to read it as that customer and name what they wouldn't say or do. Apply the fixes, tagged `[team]`.

6. **Quick test.** Play the user (mode 4) and take one question: "Walk me through last week." Two minutes, then step out with feedback. If the team says it doesn't sound real, go back to step 5.

## Done when

- [ ] `panel/evidence.md` and `panel/icp.md`
- [ ] a buyer, a user and a sceptic, each read and corrected by someone on the team
- [ ] fewer than a third `[assumed]` per persona, or the gap that causes it named

Finish with the checklist, the reminder that personas are hypotheses, not evidence, and: "Next session: `/ai-product:06-interview`."

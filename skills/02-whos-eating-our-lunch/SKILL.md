# 02 · Who's eating our lunch?

**Goal.** See who in the product's market is doing what with AI, and at which level, from System of Record to System of Understanding. Know who is already attacking, and where the openings are.

Work in the team's product folder: the current folder, or the folder below it that has `product.md`. If there is none, run `/ai-product:start` first. Remind the team they can add files to `documents/` at any time, and read any new ones. Read `product.md` and `01-brief.md`.

## Steps

1. **Scope the market (2 minutes).** From `01-brief.md`, propose 10–15 companies in three groups: incumbents, AI-native challengers, horizontal players. Ask the team what's missing and add it.

2. **Run both at once.** Launch in parallel:
   - the `ai-level-map` skill on the market, writing `02-ai-market-map.md` and `02-ai-market-map.html`;
   - the `threat-scan` skill on the product, writing `02b-threats.md`.

   Split the team: half reads the level map as it lands, half the threats. Flag anything they know is wrong; tag those lines `[human]`.

3. **Place our product.** Ask the team where their product sits today, then apply the level rules and show where the evidence puts it. If the two differ, write both and why.

4. **Connect the two.** Add the named attackers from `02b-threats.md` to the map if they're missing, and mark which level each attacks from.

5. **Openings and the agent question.** With the team, name one opening: an empty or thin level where the product could lead. Then ask, and write their answer in their words:

   > If an agent sat on top of our data tomorrow, what would customers stop paying us for?

Tell the team to open `02-ai-market-map.html` in a browser.

## Done when

- [ ] at least ten companies placed, each with a source, or listed as not placed
- [ ] the team's product placed with the same rules
- [ ] the most credible attacker named, with a source
- [ ] one opening named, and the agent question answered

Finish with the checklist and: "Next session: `/ai-product:03-read-the-domain`."

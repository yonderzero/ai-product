# 01 · Know your product

**Goal.** A sourced brief of the product that the team has corrected, the idea they came in with written down, and one line on what AI should do for the product.

Work in the team's product folder. Read `product.md`; if it's missing, run `00-setup` first.

## Steps

1. **The idea.** Copy `templates/idea.md` to `inputs/idea.md` (unless it exists) and ask the team to fill it in while the research runs. It comes back at the shortlist in session 08.

2. **The brief.** Use the `company-brief` skill on the product from `product.md`, scoped to the **product, not the company**: what it does, who buys it and who uses it, the workflows it runs, pricing, tech, competitors and alternatives. Leave out company financials and people unless they bear on the product. Save it as `01-brief.md`. Tell the team to start reading as soon as the first sections land.

3. **Fact-check.** Ask each person on the team to name one fact that is wrong, stale or missing. Apply each correction and tag the line `[human]`. Push for at least one per person.

4. **The ambition.** Ask the team for one line and put it at the top of `01-brief.md`:

   > Our product's AI ambition: … · save or earn

   *Save*: the team or its customers do the same work faster or cheaper. *Earn*: a new product, tier, module or better price. Offer a draft if they're stuck, but the line is theirs.

## Done when

- [ ] `01-brief.md` exists, with sources
- [ ] at least one `[human]` correction per team member
- [ ] the ambition line, marked save or earn
- [ ] `inputs/idea.md` filled in

Finish with the checklist, ticked, and: "Next session: `/product-track:02-whos-eating-our-lunch`."

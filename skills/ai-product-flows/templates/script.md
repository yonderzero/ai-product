# [Product] · Customer interview kit

For interviewing real customers about [the problem] and the flows, using the mockups. About their life and the past first, the mockups second. Built on [date].

**Who to talk to:** [personas, and how many of each] · **Bring:** the mockups in `mockups/`

## Before you start

- Ask permission to take notes or record. Don't keep names or contact details in the notes you share.
- You're there to learn, not to sell. Talk less than they do.

## Part 1 · Their world (no mockups yet)

The riskiest assumptions from `../problem/problem.md`, as questions about the past:

1. "Tell me about the last time [the problem] happened."
2. "What did you do about it? What did that cost you?"
3. "What have you tried to fix it? Why didn't it work?"
4. "Who else gets involved when it goes wrong?"
5. …

## Part 2 · The flows

Show each flow's mockup one moment at a time. Don't explain it first.

1. "What do you think this is?"
2. "What would you do first here?"
3. "What would you let this do on its own? What would you always want to check?"
4. "What's missing for you to rely on it?"
5. "What would this replace in your week?"

## Part 3 · Commitment

Not "would you buy it?", but a real next step:

- "Could we try this with your team on [one real case]?"
- "Who else should see this?"
- "Who would need to agree to pay for something like this?"

## After the interview

Write notes the same day, in their words, and put them in `documents/`. Then run `/ai-product-flows` again to compare them with the panel.

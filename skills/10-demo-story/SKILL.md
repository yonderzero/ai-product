# 10 · Friday's demo story

**Goal.** The story the team tells on Friday while the software runs, with no slides, and a handoff the developers can work from without the product team in the room.

Work in the team's product folder. Read `handoff/` and `09-challenge.md`.

## Friday's rules

A live demo of working software, with no slides. It must cover the product, the customer, pricing, revenue impact and the path forward. Dashboards and chatbots are not accepted. Revenue-generating ideas are preferred, with a bonus for agentic AI.

## Steps

1. **The story.** Fill `templates/10-demo-story.md` into `10-demo-story.md`: five beats, about a minute each, with what to say, what's on screen, and the file each claim comes from. Add the ask (budget, people, sponsor, start date) and the questions the panel is likely to ask, each with a short answer from the files.

2. **Read it aloud.** Ask one person to read it out loud while another times it. Cut until it fits in five minutes.

3. **The handoff check.** Check `handoff/` against the ready-to-build list in `templates/README-handoff.md`. For anything missing, name an owner and a time.

4. **The README.** Fill `templates/README-handoff.md` into `handoff/README.md`, including the developers' open questions and who answers them.

## Done when

- [ ] the demo story fits in five minutes, read aloud
- [ ] every ready-to-build item is there or has an owner
- [ ] `handoff/README.md` written

Finish with the checklist. On Thursday and Friday, the team answers the developers' questions, tests each build against the prototype and the acceptance criteria, and keeps `10-demo-story.md` current.

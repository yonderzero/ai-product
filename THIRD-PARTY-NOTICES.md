## Ideas, not code

- `synthetic-customers` follows ideas from persona-plugin (https://github.com/IrrealityLabs/persona-plugin, MIT): isolated per-persona agents, calibration against real answers, a per-persona evidence log, Van Westendorp pricing. No code or text is copied.
- Its persona record groups fields the way MatrAIx's persona schema does (background, psychology, capability, behaviour). No MatrAIx data is used.
- Buyer fields follow Adele Revella's Five Rings of Buying Insight.

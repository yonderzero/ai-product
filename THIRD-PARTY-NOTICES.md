# Third-party notices

## Ideas, not code

- `ai-product-research` follows ideas from persona-plugin (https://github.com/IrrealityLabs/persona-plugin, MIT): isolated per-persona agents, calibration against real answers, a per-persona evidence log, and the panel modes. No code or text is copied.
- The persona record groups fields the way MatrAIx's persona schema does (background, psychology, capability, behaviour). No MatrAIx data is used.
- The buyer fields follow Adele Revella's Five Rings of Buying Insight.
- The price vote uses Peter van Westendorp's price sensitivity meter (1976).
- `ai-product-problem` uses Jobs to be Done (Clayton Christensen, Bob Moesta), the job map (Tony Ulwick), the four forces of progress (Bob Moesta), the Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick) and the BHAG (Jim Collins and Jerry Porras), described in our own words.

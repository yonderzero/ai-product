How each type of customer experiences the problem in `../problem/problem.md`, and the options they have today. Built from public sources, `../documents/` and the product manager's answers on 27 Oct 2026. Every claim has its source.

**The problem:** when a truck signals it needs work, through a fault code, a driver's defect or a PM coming due, the maintenance manager wants to know which trucks need work today and have each job ready to start, with the parts and a slot that fits dispatch, so they can keep every truck safe and on the road when dispatch needs it.

**What no option does well today:** taking a signal all the way to a ready job in the fleet's own shop. Telematics bundles turn a code into a work order, and Fleetio approves repairs within limits, but nobody picks the few trucks that matter across codes, defects and PMs, gets the parts in, finds a slot that survives dispatch, and captures what the technician found. Trust is the other gap: half of fleets in Fleetio's survey name accuracy and reliability as their main barrier to AI (https://www.government-fleet.com/news/report-finds-53-3-of-fleets-researching-or-piloting-ai-capabilities).

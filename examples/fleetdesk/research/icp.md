## Who is not our ideal customer, and why

- **Fleets under 75 vehicles.** We win a third of those deals and they make up 61 of 88 churned customers; they buy bundles on price [documents/lost-deals-h1-2026.md] [documents/churn-note-2026.md].
- **Fleets without their own shop.** The job runs through bays and parts rooms they don't have; outsourced-repair tools such as ServiceUp fit them better [source: https://www.serviceup.com/blog/serviceup-launches-the-agentic-repair-platform-for-modern-fleets].
- **Customers without a telematics feed.** About 500 of our customers have no codes flowing in, so the first signal is only defects and PMs [documents/fault-code-assist-beta-feedback.md].
- **Government fleets.** A different sale, through public procurement [PM, illustrative].

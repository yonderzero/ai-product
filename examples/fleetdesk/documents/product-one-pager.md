# FleetDesk: fleet maintenance that keeps trucks on the road

*Fictional. FleetDesk's sales one-pager, 2026 edition.*

FleetDesk is fleet maintenance software for trucking and delivery fleets that run their own shops. Since 2011 we've helped fleets plan preventive maintenance, run work orders, manage parts and stay ready for DOT audits. Today about 800 fleets manage more than 115,000 trucks, tractors, vans and trailers with FleetDesk.

## What you get

- **Preventive maintenance:** schedules by miles, engine hours or days, with meter readings synced from Samsara, Motive and Geotab.
- **Work orders:** from driver defect to closed repair, with labor, parts, VMRS codes and technician sign-off, on desktop or tablet.
- **Driver inspections (DVIR):** pre- and post-trip inspections in the FleetDesk Driver app; defects go straight to the shop.
- **Parts and purchasing:** inventory across shops, reorder points, purchase orders, and vendor price history.
- **Outside repairs:** record vendor repairs and invoices against each vehicle.
- **Compliance:** DOT annual inspection records, inspection and repair records kept for each vehicle, audit-ready reports.
- **Reports:** cost per vehicle, PM compliance, technician productivity, parts usage.
- **Fault Code Assist (beta):** explains engine fault codes in plain English, rates how urgent they are and suggests a VMRS repair code.

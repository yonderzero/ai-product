# Customer interview: director of fleet operations, foodservice distributor

*Fictional. Notes from a customer call by the Wrenchly product team, 23 Sep 2026. Names removed.*

**Who:** director of fleet operations at a regional foodservice distributor. 180 vehicles: 110 refrigerated box trucks and 70 tractors with reefer trailers, across three depots. One central shop with 6 technicians; the two smaller depots use outside vendors and a mobile repair service. Wrenchly customer since 2021, on the Core plan. Trucks run Motive telematics. Signs the Wrenchly contract and owns the maintenance budget.

**Interviewers:** product manager, customer success manager.

## Their world

- Every truck that doesn't roll in the morning means missed deliveries and a phone call from a restaurant. "A truck down for a day costs me more than Wrenchly costs me for a year for that truck."
- Reports cost per mile, downtime and maintenance spend to the CFO every month. Builds it in Excel from a Wrenchly export, the fuel card file and vendor invoices. Takes "two days a month, every month".
- Measured on: on-time delivery, vehicle availability at 5 am, maintenance cost per mile, and DOT and food-safety audits (reefer units).

## What works

- One place for every work order, including outside vendors.
- Drivers do their pre-trip DVIR on the Wrenchly app. Defects go straight to the shop.

## What hurts

- **Outside vendor repairs.** Half their repair spend goes to outside vendors. Invoices arrive by email and fax, each with different line items. "I have no idea if the dealer in Joliet charges me twice what the one in Gary does for the same brake job."
- **Parts waiting.** Trucks sit waiting for parts because nobody ordered ahead of the PM. "We know the PM is due. We know which filters it takes. Why am I waiting on filters?"
- **Reports.** The cost-per-mile report the CFO wants doesn't exist in Wrenchly. He rebuilds it monthly.
- **Too many logins.** Motive for drivers and GPS, Wrenchly for maintenance, the fuel card portal, the parts vendor's site. "Motive's rep says they can do the maintenance piece too and I'd have one login. I haven't said no."
- **Reefer units.** Refrigeration unit maintenance is tracked in a spreadsheet, outside Wrenchly, because the reefer hours don't come through.

## Quotes

- "I don't need AI that talks to me. I need trucks ready at 5 am."
- "If Wrenchly could tell me which vendor is overcharging, it would pay for itself in a quarter."
- "Price per truck is fine. I'd pay more if it cut downtime and I could prove it to the CFO."

- Customer success to flag renewal risk: renewal in March 2027; Motive bundling offer on the table.

# Churn note: October 2025 to September 2026

*Fictional. Prepared by Wrenchly customer success for the leadership team, October 2026.*

## Summary

- Customers at the start of the period: 815. Lost: 88 (10.8% logo churn). Won: 85. Customers at the end: 812.
- Revenue retention: gross 90%, net 101% (price increase and added vehicles at larger customers offset the losses).
- Churn is concentrated in fleets under 75 vehicles: 61 of the 88.

## Why they left

| Reason | Customers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Moved to telematics vendor's bundled maintenance (Samsara, Motive) | 31 | Decided by finance or operations, not maintenance. Most had fewer than 100 vehicles. |
| Acquired, merged or closed | 19 | The freight downturn hit small carriers. |
| Mobile app and ease of use | 14 | Technicians and drivers found the app slow; several said "too many clicks to close a work order". |
| Price | 12 | Mostly the 2026 list-price increase (Core from $7.50 to $8 per vehicle per month). |
| Moved to Fleetio | 8 | Simpler, better mobile. |
| Other | 4 | |

## What keeps customers

- Larger fleets with their own shops rarely leave: churn above 150 vehicles was 4%.
- Customers using parts inventory in two or more shops: churn 3%.
- Customers who pass a DOT audit with Wrenchly reports tend to mention it at renewal.

## Watch list

- 37 customers have a telematics vendor offering bundled maintenance at renewal (customer success estimate).
- 9 of the top 50 customers asked about AI in their last business review; none has an AI budget line for maintenance yet.

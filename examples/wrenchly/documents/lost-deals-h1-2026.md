# Sales notes: won and lost deals, first half of 2026

*Fictional. Summary by Wrenchly's head of sales for the product team, July 2026. Customer names removed.*

## The numbers

- 148 qualified deals decided in January–June 2026. Won 64 (43%). Lost 84.
- Average new customer: 120 vehicles, Core plan, first-year contract value about $13,000.
- Win rate by fleet size: 51% for 150–500 vehicles; 34% for under 75 vehicles.

| Lost to | Deals | What we heard |
|---|---|---|
| Fleetio | 29 | Easier to use, better mobile app, faster to set up, cheaper for small fleets. "They were live in a week." |
| Samsara (maintenance bundled with telematics) | 21 | One vendor, one bill, one login for the CFO. Often decided above the maintenance manager. |
| Whip Around | 9 | Driver inspections first; maintenance "good enough" for smaller fleets. |
| Motive (bundled) | 8 | Same as Samsara: bundled with ELD and dashcams. |
| No decision / stayed on spreadsheets | 11 | Couldn't justify the change; "our Excel works". |
| Other | 6 | Dealer-provided tools, a homegrown system, one ERP module. |

## Why we win

- Multi-shop parts inventory and purchasing: deeper than Fleetio and the telematics bundles.
- DOT audit-ready records and reports: maintenance managers trust them.
- Our people: implementation specialists who ran fleet shops themselves.
- Fleets with their own shops and 150+ vehicles, where the maintenance manager has a say.

## What came up in demos

- Every second demo now gets the question "What does your AI do?" We show the Fault Code Assist beta. Prospects like it but ask why it doesn't open the work order itself.
- Two prospects showed us Samsara's and Fleetio's AI features in their own demos. We had no answer for automatic work-order notes.
- Buyers above 150 vehicles ask for cost-per-mile benchmarks: "How do we compare to fleets like us?"

## Ask to product

1. Faster setup for small fleets, or stop chasing them.
2. A clear answer to "one bill with telematics": partner, bundle or out-value.
3. Show AI doing work, not just explaining codes.

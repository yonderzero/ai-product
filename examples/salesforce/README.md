# Worked example: Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud

> **Illustrative example built from public sources on 27 Oct 2026. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Salesforce. The product manager's answers, the vision, the personas and the panel are invented to show how the skills work. Salesforce, Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Einstein and Agentforce are trademarks of Salesforce, Inc.**

This folder shows what a product manager has after running all seven skills, in order, with Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud as the product:

1. `/ai-product-start` builds a sourced profile of the product and its customers.
2. `/ai-product-vision` places the product's AI features on six levels, from System of Record to System of Understanding, maps the market and who is moving in, ranks opportunities and turns them into a vision.
3. `/ai-product-discovery` scores what protects the product (3.67), how strong it is (3.83), the AI opportunity (5.0) and the exposure (2.64), with AI urgency immediate; checks readiness, maps where value flows, and chooses the move: Build on it.
4. `/ai-product-problem` says the problem as a customer job, grades the evidence, thinks bigger with a BHAG and picks the right problem from three framings: sales managers can't see where every deal stands without chasing reps.
5. `/ai-product-research` goes deep on each customer type, defines the ideal customer, builds four synthetic personas, works out the double cost (licence plus people's time), uncovers five agentic customer experiences and picks one to explore first: the forecast call without the chase.
6. `/ai-product-flows` maps each experience as a flow, makes the flows to take forward clickable, tests them with the synthetic panel in two rounds, and chooses the right flows: the rep confirms the deal, and the manager's forecast brief is built only from what reps confirmed.
7. `/ai-product-roadmap` turns those flows into prioritised features, works out the ROI for a typical customer and for Salesforce, chooses the price ($45 a user a month), and lays out Now, Next and Later toward the vision and the BHAG, with the build-day slice for developers.

Facts come from public pages cited by URL. Anything a real product manager would have supplied is tagged `[PM, illustrative]`. There is no `documents/` folder and there are no real customer interviews: the panel is synthetic, and its results are hypotheses, not evidence.

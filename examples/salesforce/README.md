# Worked example: Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud

> **Illustrative example built from public sources on 27 Oct 2026. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Salesforce. The product manager's answers and the vision are invented to show how the skills work. Salesforce, Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Einstein and Agentforce are trademarks of Salesforce, Inc.**

This folder shows what a product manager has after running `/ai-product-start`, `/ai-product-vision`, `/ai-product-discovery`, `/ai-product-problem` and `/ai-product-research`, using Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud as the product. The first skill builds a sourced profile of the product and its customers. The second places the product's AI features on six levels, from System of Record to System of Understanding, maps the market on the same levels, maps who is moving in, finds and ranks opportunities, and turns it all into a vision. The third scores what protects the product, how strong it is, the AI opportunity and the exposure, checks readiness for the vision, maps where value flows, chooses the move and sets where to take the product. The fourth takes the problem discovery chose, says it as a customer job, grades the evidence with the Mom Test, pressure-tests it, thinks bigger with a BHAG, and lays out three sized framings so the product manager picks the right problem. The fifth goes deep on how each type of customer experiences that problem and the options they have today, defines the ideal customer, builds four synthetic personas with every field sourced, works out what the product costs a typical customer in licence and in people's time, uncovers five agentic customer experiences, and lets a synthetic panel pressure-test them. Facts come from public pages cited by URL. Anything a real product manager would have supplied is tagged `[PM, illustrative]`.

# Worked example: Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud

> **Illustrative example built from public sources on 27 Oct 2026. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Salesforce. The product manager's answers and the vision are invented to show how the skills work. Salesforce, Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Einstein and Agentforce are trademarks of Salesforce, Inc.**

This folder shows what a product manager has after running `/ai-product-start` and then `/ai-product-vision`, using Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud as the product. The first skill builds a sourced profile of the product and its customers. The second places the product's AI features on six levels, from System of Record to System of Understanding, maps the market on the same levels, maps who is moving in, finds and ranks opportunities, and turns it all into a vision. Facts come from public pages cited by URL. Anything a real product manager would have supplied is tagged `[PM, illustrative]`.

## Files

- [`product.md`](product.md): the product profile: company, customers and four personas, workflows, pricing, competitors, data and AI today.
- [`ai-levels.md`](ai-levels.md): the value Sales Cloud and Service Cloud give at each AI level, who it's for and how they pay; level today is 4, AI Delegated.
- [`market-map.md`](market-map.md): 14 companies placed on the six levels, each with source, status, confidence and where it's heading.
- [`market-map.html`](market-map.html): the same map as a page; open it in a browser.
- [`market-moves.md`](market-moves.md): who is moving into the market, across five ways in, and what that opens up.
- [`opportunities.md`](opportunities.md): the sales and service work as nine steps, then six opportunities, rated and ranked.
- [`vision.md`](vision.md): the illustrative AI vision: target level 5 by 2028, what each higher level could look like, and a first step.

Placements are a snapshot from public sources on 27 Oct 2026. Check the ones you act on.

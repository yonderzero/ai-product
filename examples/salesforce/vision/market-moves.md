# Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud · Who's moving in your market

Snapshot from public sources on 27 Oct 2026.

**In short:** The busiest move is in customer service. Well-funded AI agent companies handle whole customer conversations and sell them per resolution, not per seat. Simpler self-serve CRMs with free plans and easy Salesforce import go after smaller teams. Microsoft and ServiceNow reach CRM buyers through products those buyers already run. This opens up three things for us: lead on outcomes in service, use Fin for the fast-setup end of the market, and make the CRM record keep itself so tools on top have less to add.

## What this opens up for us

- **Lead on outcomes in service.** Sierra and Decagon sell on resolutions. We already offer $2 per conversation and, since June 2026, the Help Agent that charges only when it resolves an issue. [https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/] [https://www.cxnetwork.com/cx-experience/news/salesforce-launches-pay-per-resolution-agent/amp]
- **Use Fin for the fast-setup customer.** Salesforce says Fin adds faster-to-deploy agents for small and some commercial businesses: the same segment Attio and Lightfield target. [https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/06/15/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-fin]
- **Make the record keep itself.** Gong, Clari and Coffee all live off data reps type in. If Sales Cloud captures activity and updates deals on its own, the gap they fill shrinks.
- **Show up where people already work.** Microsoft's pitch is Copilot in Outlook and Teams. We have Slack, the Agentforce Sales app in ChatGPT and Headless 360 for outside agents. [https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-brings-agentforce-sales-to-chatgpt/] [https://www.destinationcrm.com/Articles/CRM-News/CRM-Across-the-Wire/Salesforce-Expands-Headless-360-Across-Its-Portfolio-176206.aspx]
- **Stay open to builders.** In 2025 Salesforce limited how outside AI tools such as Glean could store and index Slack data, and critics called it lock-in. Clear, open terms keep builders working on Salesforce rather than around it. [https://www.cxtoday.com/crm/salesforce-blocks-competitors-from-using-slack-data-so-what/]

## What we looked for and didn't find

- **No one selling CRM through payments, commerce or cloud platforms.** Searches for Stripe, Shopify, Google Workspace or AWS launching CRM or service agents for small businesses in 2026 found no such launch.
- **No OpenAI CRM.** Searches found only partnerships the other way: Salesforce apps inside ChatGPT and OpenAI models inside Agentforce.
- **No app that fully replaces Sales Cloud or Service Cloud.** Tools on top present themselves as additions; replacement claims come only from vendors' own marketing.
- **No proof that Sierra replaces Service Cloud.** Sources describe Sierra connecting to existing CRMs, not displacing them.

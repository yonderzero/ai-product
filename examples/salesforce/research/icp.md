## Who is not our ideal customer, and why

- **Small teams on Starter or Pro** with a handful of sellers: the founder is the forecast, and the chase is a conversation over coffee. The labour saved is too small for an add-on. `[PM, illustrative]`
- **Large enterprises with a forecasting team and Clari built into their process:** the pain is real but the habit is strong, and the sale is a multi-year replacement. Later. `[PM, illustrative]`
- **Companies that don't record calls or allow email capture,** for legal or cultural reasons: the agent has little to read. `[assumed]`

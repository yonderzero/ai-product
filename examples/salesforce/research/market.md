# Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud · The problem in the market

> Illustrative. Built from public sources; not affiliated with or endorsed by Salesforce. Anything tagged `[PM, illustrative]` is invented to show what a product manager would add.

How each type of customer experiences the problem in `../problem/problem.md`, and the options they have today. Built from public sources and the product manager's answers on 27 Oct 2026; there is no `documents/` folder in this example (`evidence.md`). Every claim has its source.

**The problem:** when the quarter is on the line and the forecast call is coming, a sales manager wants to know where every deal really stands and which ones need them, without chasing reps, so they can call the number with confidence and spend their time coaching.

## The problem through each persona's eyes

| Persona type | How they see the problem | How often, what it costs | What they do now | What they've tried | What would make them switch | What worries them |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer: sales manager or VP of sales | "I can't believe the pipeline, so I chase and rebuild it myself" | Every week, worst at quarter end; about 4 hours a week `[PM, illustrative]`; only 24% of leaders trust reps' commits ([Gong](https://www.gong.io/press/as-economic-headwinds-grow-stronger-only-24-of-sales-leaders-are-confident-in-their-teams-revenue-forecast)) | Messages reps, keeps a personal forecast sheet, runs a weekly call ([RevOps Coop](https://www.revopscoop.com/post/salesforce-forecasting)) | Validation rules, Clari or Gong on top `[PM, illustrative]` | A forecast that is current without asking, and that replaces the spreadsheet | Paying twice for the same view; reps feeling watched |
| User: account executive | "Admin that keeps my manager happy and takes me away from selling" | After every touch; about 2 hours a week of data entry ([State of Sales](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/state-of-sales-report-announcement-2026/)) | Batches updates before the call; notes elsewhere ([Scratchpad](https://www.scratchpad.com/blog/salesforce-issues), vendor view) | Activity Capture, note-takers `[PM, illustrative]` | Never typing a stage or date again, and being asked less | Wrong changes in their name; the manager seeing everything first |
| Sceptic: revenue operations or admin | "Bad data in, bad forecast out, and I'm the one who gets blamed" | Weekly clean-ups, quarterly resets ([RevOps Coop](https://www.revopscoop.com/post/salesforce-forecasting)) | Validation rules, nudging flows, bulk resets ([RevEngine](https://revengine.substack.com/p/automatically-nudging-reps-to-update)) | Activity Capture, whose email wasn't reportable ([Revenue Grid](https://revenuegrid.com/blog/einstein-activity-capture-limitations/), competitor) | Fewer tickets and resets, with a log of every change | AI they can't audit; another tool to own; trust over cost ([Salesforce Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/5-reasons-salesforce-professionals-distrust-ai/)) |

| Adjacent: financial advisor | "Every meeting means notes, tasks and a CRM update before the next one" | Every client meeting; more than two hours behind the scenes for every hour with clients, per Kitces research *(search result only)* | AI notetakers, now used widely ([Family Wealth Report](https://www.familywealthreport.com/printarticle.php?id=207153)) | Switching notetakers on price ([Advisor.ca](https://advisor.ca/practice/technology/what-advisors-need-to-know-about-ai-notetaking-apps/)) | Notes that land in the CRM correctly and pass compliance | Liability for errors ([Kitces](https://www.kitces.com/blog/artificial-intelligence-ai-meeting-notetakers-note-tools-client-communication-accuracy-domentation/)) |

**What no option does well today:** keep every deal current from what actually happened, with the rep confirming rather than typing, and hand the manager a forecast that replaces the spreadsheet, inside the record everyone already uses. Tools on top see the calls but depend on the record; the record holds everything but waits for reps to type.

## What we looked for and didn't find

- Reddit threads and full G2, Capterra and TrustRadius reviews from Sales Cloud users: the pages couldn't be opened.
- A first-hand account of a rep hiding bad news on purpose. What exists points to optimism, pressure and lack of time.
- Any rep saying they would or wouldn't trust a record they didn't type.
- Official prices for Gong, Clari, Backstory and Salesforce Revenue Intelligence: none are published.
- A measured count of how long managers spend chasing and rebuilding the forecast.
- What service managers are measured on, from job postings.

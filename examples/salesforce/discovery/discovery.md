# Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud · Discovery

> Illustrative. The product manager's answers and the score they overturned are invented to show how `/ai-product-discovery` works, tagged `[PM, illustrative]`. Everything else cites the reports in this folder or a public source.

Is the product ready to go where the vision says? Built from `protection.md`, `strength.md`, `exposure.md`, `value.md`, `../vision/vision.md` and the product manager's answers on 27 Oct 2026.

## In short

Sales Cloud and Service Cloud are strong where it is hardest to copy: brand, partners, integrations and years of customer history. The exposure is in service, where well-funded AI-native agents are winning decisions, and in how easily data and habits can move once AI does the migration work. The move is **Build on it**: protect the record and the ecosystem, make the deal record keep itself, win service on outcomes with Fin, and earn customers' permission to learn across their data so benchmarks can follow.

## Where to take the product

- **Protect:** the record and the ecosystem around it: integrations, AppExchange and the partner network. Keep them open, so builders work on Salesforce rather than around it (`protection.md`, `exposure.md`).
- **Grow:**
  1. *The deal record that keeps itself.* Value comes from defending the seat and from taking back the work Gong, Clari and Day.ai do on top (`../vision/opportunities.md` #1, `value.md`).
  2. *Outcome-priced service with Fin for the fast-setup customer.* Value comes from per-resolution revenue in the segment AI-native agents are winning (`../vision/opportunities.md` #2).
  3. *Opt-in benchmarks, "deals like yours".* Value comes from a new subscription per sales team, built on history no newcomer has (`../vision/vision.md`, `../vision/opportunities.md` #6).
- **Start with:** reps' deal records are never current, so managers don't trust the forecast and reps lose selling time to data entry. Before building anything, learn with 10 Sales Cloud customers whether reps would trust an agent to keep records current without checking, and whether sales leaders would opt in to sharing anonymised deal data (`../vision/vision.md`). `[PM, illustrative]`
- **Don't:**
  - Don't build benchmarks before customers have opted in and the records are clean: both are prerequisites.
  - Don't spend on quoting or sales-to-success handoff agents yet: the evidence is thin (`../vision/opportunities.md`, open questions).
  - Don't reprice seats across the board until outcome pricing is proven. `[PM, illustrative]`
- **How we'll know:**
  - The share of agent-proposed updates reps accept without edits.
  - The share of sales leaders who say yes to opting in, and on what terms.
  - The service win rate against AI-native agents, and the number of customers on per-resolution pricing. `[PM, illustrative]`

## Where we're strong

- First in CRM for the 13th year, with a 20.0% share in 2025 [https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/74831/salesforce-tops-idc-global-crm-market-share-for-13th-straight-year]
- 3,800+ consulting firms and 170,000+ certified experts build on Salesforce [https://www.forrester.com/blogs/the-salesforce-consulting-landscape-shows-no-sign-of-slowdown/]
- 91% of customers run at least one AppExchange app [https://smartbridge.com/salesforce-appexchange-apps-top-20-for-2026/]
- Customers stay: revenue attrition is about 8% [https://transcripts.platformaeronaut.com/summaries/CRM-4Q26-AI-Summary]. `[PM, illustrative]` Our own renewal data matches, and most churn is among small teams that find setup heavy.
- AI already earns money: Agentforce annual recurring revenue is above $1.5B [https://investor.salesforce.com/news/news-details/2026/Salesforce-Delivers-Record-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027-Results/default.aspx]

## Where we're exposed

- **The protection eroding fastest: switching costs.** Data exports as CSV every week, and AI tools make migration, retraining and re-mapping processes cheaper (`protection.md`).
- **The highest risk: AI disruption.** Sierra raised $950M and serves over 40% of the Fortune 50 [https://www.vktr.com/customer-experience/sierra-raises-950m-at-15b-valuation-eyes-transformation-beyond-customer-support/]. `[PM, illustrative]` AI-native agents now appear in most mid-market service deals we lose.
- **Timing.** Investors are pricing the risk now: the stock was down about 33% in 2026 by late May, on fears for seat-based pricing [https://www.tikr.com/blog/salesforce-beat-q1-earnings-by-0-76-a-share-heres-why-the-stock-is-still-down-33-in-2026]
- **Trust around data.** A default-on setting that lets customer data train global predictive models drew criticism [https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforces-new-ai-data-setting-sparks-debate-over-what-customers-agreed-to/]

## The AI opportunity

- **AI in the product today:** level 4, AI Delegated (`../vision/ai-levels.md`).
- **Where AI could earn money:**
  - Per-resolution service for the fast-setup customer, with Fin and the Help Agent (`../vision/opportunities.md` #2).
  - Opt-in benchmarks, sold per sales team (`../vision/vision.md`).
- **Where AI could take work off customers:**
  - Activity logging and deal updates, forecast roll-ups, case wrap-up, and data hygiene for admins (`../vision/opportunities.md` #1, #3–#5).
  - Reps spent 70% of their time on non-selling work in 2024 (`../vision/opportunities.md`).

## Ready for the vision?

The vision: level 5 by the end of 2028, starting with the deal record that keeps itself (`../vision/vision.md`, `../vision/opportunities.md` #1).

| | What we have | The gap | Must be true | How we'll check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Data** | Years of pipeline, activity and case history per customer (`../product.md`). A Spring '26 setting covers training global predictive models [https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforces-new-ai-data-setting-sparks-debate-over-what-customers-agreed-to/] | `[PM, illustrative]` Legal reads benchmarks shown to other customers as needing explicit opt-in; the MSA alone isn't enough | Enough customers opt in, in each industry and size band, for benchmarks to mean something | Ask 20 sales leaders the opt-in question in the pilot interviews; count yes, and the terms they set |
| **Tech** | Data 360, the Atlas reasoning engine, activity capture and Headless 360 (`../product.md`) [https://www.destinationcrm.com/Articles/CRM-News/CRM-Across-the-Wire/Salesforce-Expands-Headless-360-Across-Its-Portfolio-176206.aspx] | Reliable field updates from email, calendar and calls; an anonymised pipeline across customer orgs. `[PM, illustrative]` Activity capture data sits apart from the core record today | Agents update deals accurately enough that reps stop checking each change | Pilot: share of proposed updates accepted without edits, per field |
| **Team** | `[PM, illustrative]` One product manager, an engineering lead and a designer (`../product.md`) | No data scientists or privacy engineers on this team | Cross-customer modelling and privacy review are staffed before benchmarks are built | `[PM, illustrative]` Agree staffing with the Sales Cloud lead before the pilot ends |
| **Customers** | Agents already resolve service conversations with named results (`../vision/ai-levels.md`) | Reps' trust in automatic updates is unknown; trust in data use was dented (`exposure.md`) | Reps accept updates, and leaders opt in to sharing | Pilot acceptance rate, hours saved per rep, opt-in answers |
| **Pricing** | Per-conversation, per-resolution and credit pricing exist (`../product.md`) | Salesforce moved its agents back towards seat-based deals because customers wanted predictable costs [https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/12/ai_agents_salesforce_pricing/]; benchmarks have no price yet | Sales teams pay for benchmarks on top of seats | Test two price points in the 20 interviews |

**Builds on:** brand and distribution, deep integrations, a paying AI line, and years of history that is still a latent asset (`protection.md`, `strength.md`).

**Could stop it:** customers saying no to learning across their data; AI-native rivals capturing the work first; seat revenue shrinking faster than outcome revenue grows (`exposure.md`).

## Where value flows

In short, from `value.md`:

| | Today | At the vision's target level |
|---|---|---|
| **Who gets value** | Reps, managers and service teams get one record and a console; partners and tools on top get the work around it | Sales managers get a forecast they trust and benchmarks; service managers get resolved conversations |
| **Who pays, and for what** | The company, per seat ($25–$550 per user per month), plus AI add-ons, credits or $2 per conversation [https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/] | The company, per resolution in service and a benchmark subscription per sales team `[PM, illustrative]` |
| **Where value leaks** | Reps' manual entry, spreadsheet forecasts, Gong and Clari, consulting partners, AI-native service agents | Outside assistants doing the work through Headless 360, if the value of the record moves to them |
| **Who else could capture it** | Sierra, Decagon, HubSpot in service; Gong, Clari, Day.ai in sales | Microsoft 365 Copilot and ChatGPT as the place reps work; customers' own builds |
| **What keeps it with us** | Integrations, partners, brand | Data across many customers, the workflow, trust, and distribution |

**The shift:** seats for people who type into the CRM → paying for outcomes: resolved conversations and a forecast with benchmarks.
